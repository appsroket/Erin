A LITTLE CLARITY GOES A LONG WAY

Good questions.
Clearer next steps.

New to passive real estate? Start here.

What is apartment syndication?+

It’s a way for multiple investors to pool capital to invest in an apartment property. A sponsor or operating team typically finds, finances, and manages the property, while passive investors participate according to the offering’s terms.

Do I need to manage the property?+

Passive investors generally do not handle tenants, maintenance, or daily operations. The operating team manages these responsibilities. You should still review reports and understand your rights and responsibilities in the investment documents.

Who can invest?+

Eligibility depends on the offering. Some are limited to accredited investors; others may permit sophisticated investors under specific exemptions. Erin can explain the initial process, and the offering documents establish the actual requirements.

What risks should I understand?+

Real estate investments can lose value, distributions may change or stop, and private investments are typically illiquid. Financing, vacancy, expenses, and market conditions affect results. Review all offering documents and consult qualified financial, tax, and legal advisors.

How do I learn about opportunities?+

Start with the investor questionnaire or email Erin. You can discuss your goals, experience, and whether the approach may be appropriate for you.
